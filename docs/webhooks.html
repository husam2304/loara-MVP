Operations

Webhooks

Hiro exposes two webhook endpoints, defined in routes/api.php and rate-limited to 120 requests/minute. Both verify authenticity before processing.

Endpoints

ProviderMethod & pathAuth
VapiPOST /api/webhooks/vapix-vapi-secret header vs. VAPI_SERVER_SECRET
StripePOST /api/webhooks/stripeStripe signature vs. STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET

Vapi webhook

Handled by Api\VapiWebhookController. Each request must include the x-vapi-secret header matching VAPI_SERVER_SECRET; otherwise it is rejected with 401. It routes on the message type:

message.typeAction
assistant-requestResolves the clinic from the call data and returns its assistant or squad. Declines (with a spoken error) if the clinic is over its minute limit.
tool-callsResolves the clinic/call (chat sessions key off chat.id + assistant.id), dispatches each tool through VapiToolDispatcher, logs every invocation, and returns results.
status-updateCreates or updates the call record and its lifecycle status (queued → ringing → in-progress → ended).
transcriptAppends transcript lines, deduplicated by timestamp + content.
end-of-call-reportFinalizes the call: duration, cost, sentiment, AI confidence, summary, recording, and records call-minute usage.

Configure in Vapi

Set your assistants' server URL to https://your-domain.com/api/webhooks/vapi and the secret to your VAPI_SERVER_SECRET. In local development, php artisan vapi:sync-tunnel pushes your current tunnel URL to all assistants.

Stripe webhook

Handled by Api\StripeWebhookController. The raw payload and signature are verified with STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET; invalid signatures are rejected. Events are processed idempotently and keep the local subscription in sync:

EventEffect
customer.subscription.createdCreates the local subscription (plan detected from the price ID).
customer.subscription.updated / .deletedUpdates status, plan, billing period; marks cancelled.
invoice.paidRecords the invoice.
payment failureMoves the subscription to past_due.

Configure in Stripe

Add an endpoint at https://your-domain.com/api/webhooks/stripe, subscribe to the subscription and invoice events above, and copy the signing secret into STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET.

!

Webhooks require a publicly reachable HTTPS URL. Behind a firewall or on localhost, use a tunnel (Expose, ngrok) during development.

Troubleshooting

  • 401 from Vapi webhook — the x-vapi-secret doesn't match VAPI_SERVER_SECRET (or the config is cached stale; run php artisan config:cache).
  • Stripe signature failure — wrong STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET, or a proxy altered the raw body.
  • Events not arriving — confirm the endpoint is public over HTTPS and not blocked by rate limiting.
