Explore the Platform panel

Log in as hiro@mail.com. You land at /platform/dashboard. From here you can:

  • Review and enable/disable clinics, and impersonate a clinic to see what its staff sees.
  • Manage platform users and pricing plans.
  • Configure payment gateways, SMTP, Claim.MD, and branding.

See Platform Admin.

Explore the Clinic panel

Log in as clinic@mail.com. You land at /dashboard. Try:

  • Call Center — view call history and start an outbound call (requires Vapi).
  • Appointments — book, check-in, reschedule, or cancel (double-booking is prevented).
  • Patients — search the directory and add a new patient.
  • Workflow — build a multi-assistant call flow and deploy it to Vapi.
  • Settings — configure the AI assistant, operating hours, and branding.

See Clinic Panel.

Turn on the AI voice agent

The dashboard works without any keys, but to make the AI answer real calls:

  1. Add your Vapi keys to .env (see Configuration).
  2. Configure the assistant in Settings → AI Assistant and provision a phone number.
  3. Point Vapi at your webhook — https://your-domain.com/api/webhooks/vapi with the matching VAPI_SERVER_SECRET.
  4. Call the number and watch the call, transcript, and tool invocations appear in the Call Center.

Full guide: Vapi Setup.

Enable billing

  1. Add Stripe keys to .env.
  2. Define plans in Platform → Pricing and sync them to Stripe.
  3. Add the Stripe webhook pointing at /api/webhooks/stripe.

Full guide: Billing & Plans.
