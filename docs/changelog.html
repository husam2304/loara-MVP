Release history for Hiro.

v1.0.0 — June 17, 2026

Initial release

First public release of Hiro — AI-Powered Front Desk for Healthcare Clinics.

  • AI voice agent (Vapi): inbound/outbound calls, 14 server-side tools, DOB identity verification, transcripts, recordings, sentiment, and two-failure escalation.
  • Visual workflow builder: compose multi-assistant Vapi squads with transitions and one-click deploy.
  • Clinic panel: dashboard, call center, appointments (with double-booking prevention), patients, providers, triage rules, integrations, analytics, and a landing-page CMS.
  • Platform panel: clinic management with enable/disable, platform users, DB-driven pricing plans with Stripe sync, payment gateways, SMTP, and branding.
  • Billing: Stripe checkout, customer portal, plan swap/cancel/resume, trials, usage metering with plan-limit enforcement, and signed, idempotent webhooks.
  • Insurance: eligibility verification and claim submission via swappable Claim.MD / Stedi gateways.
  • Auth: multi-step clinic registration, email verification, and password reset.
  • Multi-tenancy: clinic-scoped data, six-role access model, per-plan feature flags, and impersonation (SuperAdmin → clinic, owner → staff).
  • Operations: web installer, white-label branding, custom error pages, appointment-reminder scheduler, and an extensive Pest test suite.
