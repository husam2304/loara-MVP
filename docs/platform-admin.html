Features

Platform Admin

The SaaS operator's control panel at /platform/*, available only to the SuperAdmin role. This is where you run Hiro as a business across all tenant clinics.

Dashboard

At /platform/dashboard — a top-level view of the platform: clinics, users, subscription/revenue signals, and recent activity across tenants.

Clinics

At /platform/clinics. List every clinic and open a detail view. From here you can:

  • Enable / disable a clinic. Disabling blocks its users at login and ends active sessions (via the clinic_enabled middleware).
  • Impersonate a clinic's user to see exactly what they see and provide support.

Platform users

At /platform/users — manage platform-level user accounts (other SuperAdmins / operators).

Pricing plans

At /platform/pricing. Plans are stored in the database (PlanConfiguration) and fully editable: name, description, monthly/yearly price, minute/concurrent/team limits, and feature flags. You can create, update, and delete plans, and sync a plan to Stripe to create the matching products/prices. See Billing & Plans.

Payment gateways

At /platform/payment-gateways. Configure pluggable payment gateways (Stripe by default) behind PaymentGatewayManager. Enter credentials, test the connection, and activate. Adding a gateway is a matter of implementing the gateway contract.

Settings

At /platform/settings. Global, platform-wide configuration:

SectionWhat it controls
SMTPOutgoing mail server; includes a "send test email" action.
App name / brandingThe product name shown across the UI (white-label). See White-Label & Branding.
Claim.MDInsurance clearinghouse API key, with a connection test.

Impersonation

Two impersonation flows exist, both with a clear "stop impersonating" return:

  • SuperAdmin → clinic user — initiated from /platform/clinics. Lets you operate inside a tenant for support.
  • Clinic Owner → clinic staff — initiated from the clinic's team management at /admin/users.

While impersonating, the shared impersonating Inertia prop drives a banner. Logging out restores the original administrator rather than ending the session.

!

SuperAdmins are intentionally locked out of the clinic panel (the redirect_super_admin middleware bounces them to the platform dashboard). To work inside a clinic, use impersonation.
