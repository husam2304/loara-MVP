Hiro — AI-Powered Front Desk

Hiro is a multi-tenant SaaS application that gives healthcare clinics an AI voice agent which answers calls and performs front-desk tasks — booking, rescheduling, patient lookup, insurance checks, and triage — alongside a complete clinic-management dashboard and a platform admin panel for running it as a business.

New here? Jump straight to Installation, then follow the Quick Start to log in and explore the seeded demo data.

What Hiro does

A clinic plugs Hiro into its phone line. When a patient calls, an AI voice agent (powered by Vapi) answers, verifies the caller's identity, and uses server-side tools to look up records, book or move appointments, check insurance eligibility, and assess urgency — escalating to a human when needed. Staff manage everything from a web dashboard: calls, appointments, patients, providers, insurance claims, analytics, and AI configuration.

How it is organized

Hiro is a true multi-tenant SaaS. A Clinic is the tenant and nearly every record is scoped by clinic_id. The application is split into two completely isolated panels:

  • Platform panel (/platform/*) — for the SaaS operator (SuperAdmin): clinics, platform users, pricing plans, payment gateways, and global settings.
  • Clinic panel (everything else) — for clinic staff: dashboard, call center, appointments, patients, providers, insurance, analytics, workflows, and settings.

See Architecture and Roles & Multi-Tenancy for the full picture.
