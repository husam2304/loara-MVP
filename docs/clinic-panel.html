Features

Clinic Panel

The day-to-day workspace for clinic staff. Available to clinic roles on an active subscription; some modules require specific plan features.

Dashboard

Landing screen at /dashboard. Surfaces today's snapshot — call volume, upcoming and recent appointments, missed calls, and AI performance — plus quick actions and a live notifications feed (missed calls, cancellations, upcoming appointments) shared across the app.

Call Center

At /call-center. Shows live/active calls and a paginated call log with caller, type, category, duration, and resolution. Staff can start outbound calls — single or batch — which dial through the clinic's Vapi assistant. Call status can be polled live from Vapi. Requires a configured Vapi assistant and phone number; outbound calls respect the plan's minute limit. See AI Voice Agent.

Appointments

At /appointments with day and week views. Staff can:

  • Book an appointment (patient, provider, type, time) — a conflict check prevents double-booking a provider.
  • Check in scheduled/confirmed appointments.
  • Reschedule (also conflict-checked) and cancel with a reason.

The page also shows waitlist entries and stats (today's count, remaining, no-show rate vs. the prior period, average duration).

Patients

At /patients. Search the directory by name, email, or phone and filter by status. Add Patient opens a form to create a record; Patient detail shows demographics, allergies, active medications, latest vitals, primary insurance, upcoming appointments, and a combined timeline of past visits and AI calls.

Providers

At /providers. Create, edit, and deactivate providers (doctors). Providers carry schedules and block times that feed appointment availability and the AI's check_schedule tool.

Triage rules feature: triage

At /triage-rules. Define rules that classify calls by urgency: a name, priority, action (transfer, callback, alert, voicemail), and trigger keywords by category. Rules can be created, edited, toggled active/inactive, and deleted. They power the AI's assess_urgency tool and the escalation paths shown alongside.

Integrations feature: integrations

At /integrations. Register third-party connections (EHR, payments, etc.), connect/disconnect them, and run a Sync that records an entry in the integration's sync log (audit history). See connectors note for extending sync with real provider APIs.

Analytics

At /analytics. Real metrics computed from the clinic's data: call volume over time, AI resolution rate, average handle time, satisfaction, call categories, and top intents. Includes CSV export. Advanced analytics access is gated by the advanced_analytics feature.

Landing-page CMS

At /landing-page (clinic owner). Edit the clinic's public marketing page section by section — hero, features, how-it-works, stats, pricing, CTA, footer, and example workflows — without touching code.

Settings

At /settings (clinic owner). Configure:

  • Clinic profile — name, contact, address, timezone.
  • Logo & favicon uploads (validated image types and size).
  • Operating hours — per-day open/close, used by the AI to know when the clinic is open.
  • AI Assistant — the full Vapi assistant configuration and phone number (see Vapi Setup).
  • Reminder and notification preferences.

Team management

Clinic owners manage staff at /admin/users — create, update, and remove users within their clinic, and impersonate a staff member to troubleshoot (see impersonation).
