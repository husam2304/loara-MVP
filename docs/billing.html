Features

Billing & Plans

Hiro bills clinics with Stripe (direct integration, no Cashier). Plans are database-driven and editable in the platform panel. Subscriptions, checkout, the customer portal, usage metering, and plan-limit enforcement are all built in.

Plans

Plans live in the PlanConfiguration table and are managed at Platform → Pricing. The default seed ships four tiers:

PlanHighlights
starterAI calls, appointment booking, basic analytics.
growth+ advanced analytics, SMS, integrations.
professional+ triage, campaigns.
enterprise+ custom workflows, priority support; unlimited minutes/seats.

Each plan defines price (monthly/yearly), minute/concurrent/team limits, and feature flags. A limit of -1 means unlimited.

Stripe setup

  1. Add keys to .env: 
    PAYMENT_GATEWAY=stripe
STRIPE_KEY=pk_live_…
STRIPE_SECRET=sk_live_…
STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET=whsec_…
  2. Create / sync plans in Platform → Pricing. "Sync to Stripe" creates the products and prices and fills the STRIPE_PRICE_* IDs.
  3. Add the Stripe webhook pointing at /api/webhooks/stripe (see below).

Subscription lifecycle

Handled by SubscriptionService and surfaced on the clinic's Billing page:

ActionBehavior
CheckoutCreates a Stripe Checkout session and redirects. On return, if the subscription hasn't synced yet the page shows a "finalizing" state and polls until the webhook lands.
Customer portalOpens Stripe's hosted portal to manage payment methods and invoices.
Swap planChanges plan with proration.
CancelCancels at period end (access retained until then).
ResumeReactivates a cancelled-at-period-end subscription.
TrialNew signups start in trialing for the configured trial length (config/subscriptions.php).

A grace period after past_due keeps access alive briefly (configurable) before service is gated by the subscribed middleware.

Usage metering

UsageService records AI call minutes per clinic and compares against the plan's minutes_limit:

  • Call minutes are recorded from the Vapi end-of-call-report.
  • Outbound calls are blocked when the limit is exceeded.
  • Inbound calls are declined at the assistant-request step for over-limit clinics, with a spoken message.
  • A limit of -1 (Enterprise) means unlimited — never blocked.

Usage and remaining minutes are shown on the Billing page.

Stripe webhooks

Hiro listens at:

POST https://your-domain.com/api/webhooks/stripe

Signatures are verified with STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET and events are processed idempotently. Handled events keep the local subscription in sync — creation, updates/cancellation, invoice paid, and payment failure (which moves the subscription to past_due). Billing periods are read in a way compatible with current Stripe API versions. Full detail in Webhooks.

Multiple gateways

Payments go through PaymentGatewayManager, so additional gateways can be added by implementing the gateway contract and configuring them in Platform → Payment Gateways. Stripe is the default and reference implementation.
